Education Gives Knowledge. Sanskar Gives Character.

A cultural heritage experience—not just ordinary decoration. Every installation communicates a value and invites children to practise it.

Grand Sanskar Entrance

A traditional welcome arch with torans, bells, diya motifs, floral garlands, heritage-style pillars and warm lighting. A child greeting elders with folded hands or touching grandparents’ feet symbolises respect and humility.

Ten Pillars of Sanskar

Ten attractive boards or standees, each featuring a Sanskrit value, English and Kannada meanings, an illustration and a short message children can practise.

Seven Cultural Installations

Displays celebrating respect for parents, teachers and guests, the Guru-Shishya tradition, universal family and Indian heritage.

The Tree of Sanskar

A central interactive attraction where students write one good habit or value they promise to practise on a paper leaf and hang it on the tree.

The Ten Pillars of Sanskar

Each value becomes a simple promise children can carry into everyday life.

01 · Respect

02 · Discipline

03 · Honesty

04 · Kindness

05 · Gratitude

Be thankful for what you receive.

06 · Compassion

Understand and help others.

07 · Responsibility

08 · Patriotism

09 · Respect for Elders

10 · Unity

Together we are stronger.

Design & Execution

Keep every installation cohesive, meaningful, readable and safe.

Colour Palette

Royal maroon, saffron, cream, gold and traditional green.

Materials

MDF cut-outs, flex boards, wooden frames, artificial flowers, jute and brass-finish props.

Lighting

Warm yellow lighting, decorative lanterns and LED diyas.

Languages

Use Sanskrit + English + Kannada wherever possible.

Student Involvement

Invite students to prepare quotations, paintings, value leaves and selected cultural displays.

Visual Quality

Keep boards neat and readable; use consistent fonts and sizes; avoid overcrowding the pathway.

Safety and Access

Keep the walking route clear, secure all installations, protect electrical wiring and avoid open flames.
